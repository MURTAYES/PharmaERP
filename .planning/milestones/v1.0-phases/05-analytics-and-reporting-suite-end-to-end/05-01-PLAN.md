# Plan 05-01: Aggregation Pipelines for Dashboard KPIs, Sales Summaries, and Profit/Loss

**Phase:** 05 — Analytics & Reporting Suite (End-to-End)  
**Plan:** 01 of 04  
**Type:** Backend Analytics Service & Data Aggregations  

---

## 1. Objectives & Deliverables
1. Create `server/src/services/reportService.ts` containing timezone-aware MongoDB aggregation pipelines:
   - `getDashboardKPIs()`: Today's sales, yesterday's sales, 7-day revenue, monthly revenue, transaction count, payment method splits, alert counts, and top 5 medicines sold.
   - `getSalesSummary(query)`: Aggregated sales grouped by day/month, invoice count, discount totals, charge totals, and grand total.
   - `getSalesByItem(query)`: Aggregated sold pieces, transaction occurrences, and total revenue per medicine.
   - `getProfitAndLoss(query)`: Revenue vs Cost analysis per item and overall, with uncosted item indicators.
2. Create automated tests in `server/src/tests/reports.test.ts`.

---

## 2. Step-by-Step Implementation

### Step 1: Implement `getDashboardKPIs`
- Compute today's date range in `Asia/Dhaka` (+06:00).
- Aggregate sales volume, invoice count, payment breakdown (`cash`, `card`, `mfs`), and top-selling medicines.

### Step 2: Implement `getSalesSummary` & `getSalesByItem`
- Pipeline with `$match` on date range, `$unwind` on lines, and `$group` on itemId/day.

### Step 3: Implement `getProfitAndLoss`
- Compute revenue, cost, profit, and margin percentage. Flag lines missing purchase costs.

### Step 4: Add Automated Tests
- Unit test aggregation helper math and date range parsing.

---

## 3. Verification Criteria
- `npm --prefix server test` passes with all report tests.
