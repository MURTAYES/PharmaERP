# Plan 02-03: Stock Movement Ledger, Bucket Adjustments & Expiry/Low-Stock Alerting Engine

**Phase:** Phase 2: Inventory & Stock Management (End-to-End)
**Type:** Backend Stock Ledger, Bucket Transfers & Real-Time Alerts
**Requirements Covered:** INVT-08, INVT-09, INVT-10, INVT-11, INVT-12

## Goal
Implement the immutable `StockMovement` ledger model, Owner-only stock bucket transfer & write-off endpoints (with mandatory curated reason dropdown and custom explanation), 90/60/30-day expiry calculation service, and low-stock threshold alerting aggregation engine.

## User Stories
- As an Owner, I can transfer stock between buckets (`sellable` -> `damaged`, `sellable` -> `expired`, `damaged` -> `sellable`) or write off unusable stock, with the system enforcing a structured reason selection and detail note.
- As an Owner or Pharmacist, I can see real-time alert counts and lists for expired medicines, batches expiring in <30d, <60d, and <90d, and items whose total sellable stock is below their configured threshold.
- As an Auditor, I can view an immutable stock movement ledger recording every receiving, transfer, adjustment, and write-off.

## Implementation Details

### 1. StockMovement Model (`server/src/models/StockMovement.ts`)
- Fields:
  - `batchId` (ObjectId -> Batch, required)
  - `itemId` (ObjectId -> Item, required)
  - `type` (`'RECEIVE' | 'ADJUST_TRANSFER' | 'WRITE_OFF' | 'SALE_DEDUCT' | 'RETURN_RESTOCK'`)
  - `qtyChangePieces` (number, positive or negative)
  - `bucketFrom` (`'sellable' | 'damaged' | 'expired'`, optional)
  - `bucketTo` (`'sellable' | 'damaged' | 'expired' | 'write_off'`, optional)
  - `reasonCategory` (`'Damaged in Transit' | 'Shelf Spill/Breakage' | 'Physical Count Audit Variance' | 'Customer Return Quarantine' | 'Expired Stock Quarantine' | 'Supplier Return Prep' | 'Initial Stock' | 'Direct Adjustment'`, optional)
  - `reasonDetail` (string, required for adjustments/write-offs)
  - `userId` (ObjectId -> User, required)
  - `timestamp` (Date, default: Date.now)
- Immutability: Pre-hooks blocking updates/deletes to guarantee ledger integrity.

### 2. Stock Adjustment Controller (`server/src/controllers/stockAdjustmentController.ts`)
- Endpoints:
  - `POST /api/stock-adjustments/transfer` (Owner only):
    - Input: `batchId`, `fromBucket` (`sellable` | `damaged` | `expired`), `toBucket` (`sellable` | `damaged` | `expired`), `quantityPieces`, `reasonCategory`, `reasonDetail`.
    - Validates source bucket has sufficient quantity.
    - Atomically updates Batch balances: decrements source bucket, increments target bucket.
    - Creates `StockMovement` ledger entry.
    - Creates `AuditLog` entry.
  - `POST /api/stock-adjustments/write-off` (Owner only):
    - Input: `batchId`, `fromBucket` (`damaged` | `expired`), `quantityPieces`, `reasonCategory`, `reasonDetail`.
    - Decrements bucket balance, appends ledger entry with `bucketTo: 'write_off'`, creates `AuditLog`.
  - `GET /api/stock-movements`: Filterable ledger list by `itemId`, `batchId`, `type`, and date range.

### 3. Alerts Service & Controller (`server/src/controllers/alertController.ts`)
- Endpoints:
  - `GET /api/alerts/summary`: Real-time KPI counts:
    - `expiredCount`: Batches with `expiryDate <= today` and `qtySellable > 0`
    - `critical30Count`: Batches with `today < expiryDate <= today + 30 days` and `qtySellable > 0`
    - `warning60Count`: Batches with `today + 30 days < expiryDate <= today + 60 days` and `qtySellable > 0`
    - `notice90Count`: Batches with `today + 60 days < expiryDate <= today + 90 days` and `qtySellable > 0`
    - `lowStockCount`: Items where total sellable pieces <= `lowStockThresholdPieces`
  - `GET /api/alerts/expiring?tier=...`: Returns batches matching specific expiry tier.
  - `GET /api/alerts/low-stock`: Returns items below low-stock threshold with current stock and threshold.

### 4. Automated Tests (`server/src/tests/stockAdjustment.test.ts` & `server/src/tests/alerts.test.ts`)
- Test stock bucket transfer reduces source bucket and increases destination bucket atomically.
- Test write-off deducts from damaged/expired bucket.
- Test non-owner attempt to adjust stock receives 403 Forbidden.
- Test missing reason validation error.
- Test expiry tier classification and low stock count aggregation.
